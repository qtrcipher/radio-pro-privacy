Privacy Policy — Radio Pro

Who we are

This policy covers Radio Pro for Android distributed through HUAWEI AppGallery. The developer is Hamam Alabdulla (GCC Apps), Qatar. Contact: hamam@gccapps.com. Last updated: 2026-09-17.

Data within the app

Favorites, custom station names, search history, recently played stations, settings and station reports are stored on your device. Radio Pro does not create an app account or sync these records to our own server. A station report remains local and is not sent to us. The app has no Firebase integration, app analytics or crash-reporting service.

Connections needed for listening

The app requests catalog updates from gccapps.com and sends search terms and filters to the Radio Browser directory when using online search. Selecting a directory station sends its station identifier to report a click, at most once per day per station. Catalog, directory, artwork and stream hosts receive your IP address and ordinary request data; a request may reveal the station you selected. Keeping history locally does not mean all listening data stays on your device.

Huawei advertising and your choices

We use Huawei Ads Lite and Huawei Consent for banner advertising and consent choices. Huawei may process advertising and device identifiers, including OAID; device, app and network information; IP address and approximate region; ad interactions; consent records and diagnostics. Huawei service processing depends on your device, account settings and permissions. Radio Pro does not request precise-location permission or supply search terms or listening history in its ad requests.

Personalized and basic ads

When ad options are shown, you can choose personalized or basic ads, or close the dialog to pause ads for now. Basic ads are not personalized but still process device and ad data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Review your choice and partner policies in Settings → Ad Privacy Options. Closing those options clears the consent choice and stops the app's ad requests for now; options may appear again later. You can also use Huawei device ad settings. These controls do not erase data already processed. App banners are removed only while a Pro subscription is active; stations may include their own advertising in broadcasts.

Purchases

Huawei IAP processes Huawei account, device, network, order and payment information for subscriptions, restoration, management and fraud prevention. The app receives signed purchase records to verify access. We do not receive your full payment-card details or Huawei account password. Huawei may process paying status and OAID for ad measurement under its policy and your choices. Radio Pro has no purchase server or user database.

Purposes, legal bases and other providers

Necessary processing supports the functions you request. We rely on consent where required for personalized advertising or optional processing, legitimate interests where applicable for security and support, and legal obligations for required records. Other providers include Huawei and its advertising/payment partners, Radio Browser, stream/artwork/catalog hosts, and GitHub Pages when you open the published policy. Providers may act as independent controllers; their regional policies govern their own services. Current ad-partner policies are available in Ad Privacy Options.

Retention, security and international transfers

Local records remain until you remove them, clear app data or uninstall; history is bounded and cached files can be replaced. We retain support messages as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Huawei and other providers apply the retention periods in their policies. Uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions or erase their records. Data may be processed outside your country under providers' arrangements and applicable safeguards. Catalog and artwork use HTTPS, but some station streams use HTTP; not all traffic is encrypted. Standard app backup is disabled; behavior of every device-transfer tool is not guaranteed.

Your rights and contact

Remove saved stations and clear search history in the app, or clear its storage in Android settings to remove all local app data. Email hamam@gccapps.com to request access, correction, deletion or portability of support data we hold, or to object or withdraw consent where applicable. We cannot retrieve your local history from your device. For Huawei data, use your account Privacy Center or https://www.huawei.com/en/personal-data-request. You may contact the relevant data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Children and policy changes

Radio Pro is a general-audience app, not a service directed at children. Stations determine their live content, which may not suit every age; the app has no parental filter. Contact us if a child's data was sent to us without required consent. We update this date when the policy changes and provide notice or request fresh consent when necessary.
